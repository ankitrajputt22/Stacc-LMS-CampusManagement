# User Guide

Stacc shows each person only what their roles allow; an account with several roles sees each part.

## Students

- **Dashboard and To-Do:** your courses, upcoming work, and what needs doing (assignments to submit). Dismiss what you have handled.
- **Academics:** register for courses in the registration period, and see attendance, published results, and My progress (your own figures only).
- **Courses:** work through modules and pages (Mark as complete), download files, submit assignments, take quizzes, read grades, join discussions, and message your faculty privately.
- **Calendar:** academic dates and deadlines, your own entries, a month view, and a private subscription link for your phone's calendar app.
- **Hostel:** apply (add a preference), see your queue position and room, ask for gate passes and leave, show the pass as a QR code at the gate, and report room problems.
- **Campus:** join clubs and sports, register for events (a full event puts you on the waitlist), book facilities, request campus services, and read notices.
- **ID card, Documents, Career:** your digital ID; your certificates and uploads, with certificate requests; your career profile, resumes, applications, and offers.
- **Support and Inbox:** raise tickets, reopen a resolved one within 7 days, rate the help; choose what reaches your Inbox and email.

## Faculty

- **Teaching:** create and publish your course spaces, build modules, pages, files, the syllabus, assignments with submission rules, rubrics, outcomes, the question bank, and quizzes.
- **Grading:** grade each submission with or without a rubric and move to the Next or Previous student; mark quiz answers; keep the gradebook; read course analytics and export them.
- **Classes:** take attendance, record official results, and read class lists.
- **Communication:** answer discussions (pin, accept answers) and student messages.
- **Advising:** approve the events of clubs you advise.

## Admins

- **Manage:** users (with CSV import), academic structure, offerings and enrollments, the calendar, heads of department, hostel management (wardens, bed map, occupancy), campus management (services, notices, clubs and sports, facilities, events), certificates, and support teams.
- **Oversight:** reports and Excel exports, the audit log, the helpdesk queue, and approval tasks.

## Staff

- **Warden:** your hostels' occupancy and bed map, check-ins, gate pass and leave decisions, overdue students, and ID checks.
- **Security:** the Gate page (scan or type a pass code, record going out and coming back, look students up) and ID checks.
- **Maintenance and librarian:** tickets routed to your team; librarians also check IDs.
- **Placement officer:** the placement office (companies, opportunities, rounds, applicants, offers, policy, and export).
- **Head of department:** decide the approval steps assigned to you.
